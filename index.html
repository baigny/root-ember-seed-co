About Us

Started in a truck bed. Still a little feral.

Anzacs started when our founder, a plant pathologist turned market farmer, got tired of catalog seed that performed great in a lab and folded in real soil.

Twelve years later we're a full seed house — but the standard hasn't moved: every crop variety in our vault is grown out, trialed, and re-selected on our own trial plots before it ever reaches your mailbox. We're not trying to be the biggest seed company. We're trying to be the one you trust with your soil.

  • 100% open-pollinated & non-GMO
  • Grown on regenerative trial plots
  • Small batches, tested every season
